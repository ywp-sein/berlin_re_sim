# Visualization Values

The game should show both market outputs and the forces behind them. This keeps
the simulation from becoming only a price chart.

## Top-Level Metrics

- median rent per sqm
- median purchase price per sqm
- vacancy rate
- average displacement stress
- average individual income
- average rent burden
- purchase affordability years
- share of regulated units
- share of units shifted toward sale

## Area Metrics

- demand pressure
- rent per sqm
- purchase price per sqm
- vacant units
- average household rent burden
- average displacement stress
- ownership mix: private, company, cooperative, public

## Unit Metrics

- rent
- estimated sale price
- sqm
- regulated or market-rate
- vacant or occupied
- household stress
- conversion pressure from rental to sale

## Policy Levers

- rent regulation intensity
- vacancy enforcement
- public acquisition
- investor pressure
- future: zoning capacity
- future: social housing quota
- future: renovation subsidy
- future: transaction tax

## Useful Game Feedback

The UI should explain why a value changed. Good event examples:

- a vacant unit returned to the rental market
- a household was displaced
- a unit shifted toward the purchase market
- public ownership reduced local price pressure
- investor pressure increased sale-price growth
